What happens, step by step, when you come in for a facial? First, you talk. Your provider asks what bothers you most, what you use at home, and what has worked or not worked before. Then they look at your skin up close, checking dryness, oil, spots, breakouts, and rough patches. Next comes the facial itself. There is a gentle wash, a light step to lift off dull skin, and care for clogged pores. Then come masks and serums picked for your skin, and a calm finish with moisture and sun care. You can ask questions the whole time, and you can say if anything feels too strong.

Some people worry that a facial will leave them red or sore. For most facials, that is not how it goes. Your skin may look a little pink for a few hours, and that is normal. You can go back to work or school right after. Your provider will tell you if a stronger facial needs a day of rest. Drink some water after your visit, wear sunscreen when you head back out, and skip harsh scrubs for a day or two. The glow from a facial lasts longer when you take care of your skin at home with gentle washing and a good moisturizer.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a gentle laser that helps skin look brighter, smoother, and more even. It works on sun spots, uneven tone, fine lines, and skin that just looks tired. The laser makes tiny, light touches across the skin that help old, dull skin turn over and fresh skin come through. It is a good reset for skin that has spent too many summers in the West Texas sun.

Most people feel a warm, prickly feeling during the treatment, and their skin may look pink for a day or two, a bit like a mild sunburn. After that, skin often looks clearer and feels smoother. A short series works best for most people. A facial between laser visits can keep skin calm, clean, and moist while it heals and renews.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small, gentle injections of moisture-loving ingredients that help skin hold water and look plump, soft, and bright. They are not fillers, and they do not change the shape of your face. They work under the surface to feed the skin, smooth fine lines, and bring back that healthy glow that dry West Texas air can steal away.

The treatment is quick, and most people have only tiny marks for a day or so. Results build over a few weeks as the skin drinks in the moisture. Skin Boosters work well on the face, neck, hands, and chest. If your skin feels dry no matter how much cream you use, or if it looks dull in photos, ask about pairing Skin Boosters with your next facial. The two together often give a glow that lasts longer than either one alone.

Botox

Botox softens the lines that come from years of smiling, frowning, and raising your brows. A few small, careful injections help those muscles relax just enough that the lines look softer. You can still move your face and show how you feel. You just look more rested, which is what most people want.

The visit is quick, often done during a lunch break, and most people feel only a small pinch. There is little to no downtime. Results show up over a few days and last for a few months. Many people pair Botox with a facial on a different day, so the skin looks smooth and bright at the same time. If you have never tried it, ask the team to start light. You can always add more later.

XERF

XERF is a newer kind of skin tightening treatment. It uses gentle radiofrequency energy to warm the deeper layers of your skin. That warmth tells your skin to make more collagen, the stuff that keeps skin firm and springy. Over the next few weeks, skin can look tighter and smoother, bit by bit.

What people like most about XERF is that it works without cuts, without needles, and with very little downtime. You can go back to work, pick up the kids, or head to Mackenzie Park for a walk with friends. It is a good pick for the jawline, the neck, and cheeks that have started to feel a little soft. It is not a face lift, and the team will be straight with you about that, but for mild looseness, it can make a real change.

PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro is a laser that works in tiny, fast bursts of light. It helps with dark spots, sun damage, acne marks, and fine lines. The quick bursts break up pigment into tiny bits that your body clears away on its own. Because the bursts are so fast, the skin around the spot is left alone, which means less downtime for most people.

Many folks in Lubbock come in for PicoSure Pro after years in the sun have left spots on their cheeks, hands, or chest. Spots may look darker for a few days before they fade, and that is normal. Dermal Fillers

As we get older, our faces lose a bit of the fullness we had when we were young. Cheeks can look flatter, lines around the mouth can look deeper, and lips can look thinner. Dermal fillers gently replace some of that lost fullness. They are smooth gels placed just under the skin to lift, shape, and soften. Done well, they do not change who you are. They bring back balance.

Fillers can help cheeks, smile lines, lips, and the jawline. Results show right away, and they last for months. A facial is a good way to keep the skin over the filler looking bright and healthy. The team talks with you first about what you want to see and what would look natural on your face.

Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza RF Microneedling uses tiny needles to make very small, controlled openings in the skin, while radiofrequency energy warms the deeper layers at the same time. Your skin answers by making new collagen and elastin, which help with fine lines, acne marks, large pores, and skin that feels rough or loose. It is one of the hardest working treatments on the list.

Your skin may look pink and feel warm for a day or two after, a bit like you spent too long in the sun. Most people are back to their normal routine quickly. Keralase

Keralase is a treatment for hair that looks thinner than it used to. It pairs a gentle laser with a special serum that helps the scalp and hair roots. The laser makes tiny paths that help the serum reach where it needs to go, and the serum feeds the hair roots with growth factors that support thicker, fuller looking hair over time.

Most people need a series of sessions, spaced out over weeks, to see the best change. There is little downtime, and you can wash and style your hair as usual soon after. It works best when thinning is caught early, so if you have been thinking about it for a while, it is worth asking about sooner rather than later.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

Shaving every other day gets old fast, and waxing hurts and never lasts. Titanium Laser Hair Removal treats hair at the root so it grows back finer, lighter, and slower over time. It works on larger areas like legs and backs as well as smaller spots like the upper lip and chin. A cooling tip keeps skin calm while the laser works.

Most people need a series of sessions because hair grows in rounds, and the laser works best on hair that is in its active growth phase. After each visit, hair grows back thinner. By the end of a series, many people shave far less or stop altogether. It is a smart pick before summer, before a trip, or just for the ease of not thinking about it every morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I walk in for my first facial?

Your provider looks at your skin up close, asks about the products you use at home, and builds the facial around what your skin needs that day. There is a gentle clean, a step to lift off dull skin, and then masks and moisture. You can ask questions the whole time.

How often is a facial a good idea?

For most people, once a month works well, because skin turns over about that often. If your skin is very dry or you have a big event coming up, your provider may suggest coming in sooner at first. If your skin is calm, you may stretch visits out a bit.

Will a facial help my acne or my acne marks?

A facial can help by cleaning out pores and calming skin. For acne that keeps coming back, the team may talk about other treatments too, like a gentle laser or microneedling, to help marks fade over time.

Is a facial a good choice for sensitive skin?

Yes, in most cases. Tell your provider about your skin and any products that have bothered you before. They can pick gentle steps and skip anything too strong. If your skin is very red or sore on the day of your visit, they may suggest waiting a short while.

How long will my skin stay pink after a facial?

Most facials have little to no downtime. Your skin may look a little pink for a few hours, and that is normal. You can go back to work or school right after. Your provider will tell you if a stronger facial needs a day of rest.

What makes a med spa facial different?

A med spa facial is done by trained medical staff with products and tools that work deeper in the skin. Your skin is checked closely first, and the facial is built around your needs. A salon facial is mostly about rest. Both can feel nice, but a med spa facial can do more for skin problems.
